OPTIONAL AUTONOMY

Give Wajo superpowers?

Baseline mode does not change: every reply needs your approval of the exact saved recipient, subject and text.

What changes when this is on

  • Only a qualified Draft Skill may send a matching reply automatically.
  • A Skill qualifies after two replies are sent through Wajo without changing the recipient, subject or text.
  • Permission belongs to the exact Skill revision and current Gmail account. Editing the Skill or switching accounts removes it.
  • Risk, identity, prompt-injection and irreversible-action checks can still block or escalate a reply.
  • Every automatic send is recorded in Autosent. You can remove one Skill’s permission or turn off Superpowers at any time.
